Your security, as a harbour at night

The harbour is what you protect. Attacks arrive from the open sea along three routes: email and accounts, sites and code, devices and home network. Lighthouses are security controls. Fog marks what has not been checked. Each crew member is an agent with one job; the ones shown only as a light are not built yet.

The lighthouse states on this map are a story, not a reading of any estate. Nothing here was measured.
