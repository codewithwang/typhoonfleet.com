Where the line is.

One sentence to remember: Squall closes the doors before anyone tries them. It does not chase someone who is already inside.

Before the door

Squall finds the weak spots, leaked keys, faulty parts and data leaks that let an attack start, shows proof for each, and tells you what to fix first. With nightly watch, it keeps checking as your app changes.

Behind the door

What an attacker does once inside, whether your team would notice, and how you respond and recover. For those, you need a red team exercise, monitoring and a response plan. Squall's roadmap adds alarm testing; it will never act out an attack on your systems.
